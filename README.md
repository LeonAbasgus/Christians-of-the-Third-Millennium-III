**To Christians of the Third Millennium III** is the third volume of the philosophical-theological project *To Christians of the Third Millennium (CTM)*.
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22819373

This volume presents the methodological framework of CTM analysis as a vector analysis of subjecthood.

It develops a systematic method for reconstructing acting ontologies through the analysis of:

- levels and directions of subjecthood
- Dynamic Iconic Space (DIS)
- Operating System (OS)
- distribution and bearers of subjecthood
- ontological dynamics and modes of correspondence
- ontological solvency
- correction, generativity, and scale
- negative verification and fail-closed analysis

The original language of the work is Russian.

The English edition serves as the primary international edition.
